Game Architecture

Adult psychological sci-fi visual novel & 3D real-time controllable exploration built on Ren'Py 8.x for Windows PC / Steam, with full WebGL 3D browser prototype integration.

Chapter 1 Scope & Milestones

  • Scene 1: First-person awakening in Interrogation Chamber 07, restraint struggle.
  • Scene 2: 3D controllable movement with WASD, searching filing cabinet, reading terminal, inspecting sleeping guard.
  • Scene 3: 60-Second lockdown timer, entering keypad code 4927, blast doors sliding open, corridor breach.
  • Scene 4: Observation Chamber suspension harness sequence with real pendulum physics, violent bouncing, and momentum scissor kicking.
  • Scene 5: Civilian clothing incineration and high-tech facility uniform equipment sequence.
